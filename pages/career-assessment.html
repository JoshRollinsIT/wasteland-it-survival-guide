Career Assessment

Before beginning your deployment, the Job-Boy 3000 will establish a professional profile based on your workplace strengths, choices, and accomplishments.

As you progress through training, your workplace aptitudes will grow and new career opportunities may become available.

Workplace Aptitudes

The Job-Boy continuously monitors seven core workplace aptitudes.

  • COM - Communication
  • TEAM - Teamwork
  • LEAD - Leadership
  • ORG - Organization
  • PROF - Professionalism
  • LEARN - Learning
  • ADAPT - Adaptability

Perks

Each training module contains multiple perk options.

You may choose one perk per module to define your workplace specialization.

Achievements

Every module contains an achievement that can be claimed once completed.

Achievements represent growth through experience and provide additional aptitude gains.

Training Modules

During deployment you will complete the following workplace training modules.

  • Workplace SPECIAL Assessment
    Identify and evaluate your professional strengths.
  • Interview Gauntlet
    Prepare for interviews and first impressions.
  • Communication Perks
    Build effective workplace communication skills.
  • Quest Log Management
    Learn task management and prioritization.
  • Surviving the Team
    Develop teamwork and collaboration skills.
  • Conflict Encounters
    Learn how to navigate workplace conflict.
  • Status Effects
    Recognize stress, burnout, and workplace challenges.
  • ADA Emergency Kit
    Understand accommodations and workplace resources.
  • Digital Reputation
    Build a professional online presence.
  • Endgame Advice
    Final workplace survival guidance for long-term success.
