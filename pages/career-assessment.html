Career Assessment

Before beginning your first deployment, the Job-Boy 3000 will evaluate your workplace strengths, professional habits, and career potential.

Throughout this training program you will collect perks and achievements that contribute to your professional profile. Those choices will influence the career recommendations generated at the end of your journey.

How Character Progression Works

Every training module contains workplace challenges and opportunities.

You will earn progress in two ways:

  • Perks are selected by you and represent skills you choose to develop.
  • Achievements are earned by completing modules and represent growth gained through experience.

Perks

Each training module contains multiple perk options.

You may select one perk from each training module.

Your selected perks become part of your professional build and influence your recommended career paths.

Example Perk

A perk represents a workplace skill, strength, or professional habit that helps define your character.

Achievements

Every module contains an achievement that can be claimed once completed.

Achievements provide additional aptitude bonuses and represent lessons learned through experience.

Example Achievement

Achievements can only be claimed once and remain part of your permanent professional record.

Tracked Aptitudes

The Job-Boy monitors seven workplace aptitudes during your training.

  • COM - Communication
  • TEAM - Teamwork
  • LEAD - Leadership
  • ORG - Organization
  • PROF - Professionalism
  • LEARN - Learning
  • ADAPT - Adaptability

Potential Career Paths

Different skill combinations can lead to different workplace specializations.
