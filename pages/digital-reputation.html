Mission Briefing

Every action in the digital world contributes to your professional reputation. Employers, recruiters, and potential coworkers often learn about you online before they ever meet you.

"Your resume tells employers what you've done. Your reputation tells them who you are."

Social Media Safety Check

Before You Post, Ask:

  • Would I say this in a professional meeting?
  • Would I be comfortable if a recruiter saw this?
  • Is this respectful and constructive?
  • Could this be misunderstood without context?
