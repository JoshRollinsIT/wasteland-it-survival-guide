Why Communication Matters

In IT, technical skills solve problems, but communication builds trust. Every email, ticket, meeting, and conversation affects how others view your professionalism and effectiveness.

"A technician who communicates clearly can solve problems faster than one who speaks only in technical jargon."

HR Overseer Tip

"Most workplace problems aren't caused by technology. They're caused by poor communication."
