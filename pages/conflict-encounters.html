Conflict Encounters

Not every battle requires combat. Sometimes it requires communication.

Encounter Briefing

Every workplace experiences conflict. Users get frustrated, coworkers disagree, priorities clash, and misunderstandings happen.

The goal is not to avoid every conflict. The goal is to respond professionally and work toward a solution.

"Respond to the problem. Don't react to the emotion."

HR Overseer Advisory

"Most workplace conflicts become worse because people focus on being right instead of finding a solution."

Random Encounter #1

Angry User

A user reports that their computer isn't working and begins expressing frustration.

❌ Failed Speech Check

"You need to calm down."

✔ Successful Speech Check

"I understand this is frustrating. Let's see what we can do to get the issue resolved."

Random Encounter #2

Disagreement With A Coworker

You and another team member disagree on how a problem should be handled.

❌ Failed Speech Check

"That's a terrible idea."

✔ Successful Speech Check

"Can we compare both approaches and see which option best supports the team's goals?"

Conflict Resolution Protocol

  1. Listen completely before responding.
  2. Identify the actual issue.
  3. Stay calm and professional.
  4. Focus on solutions.
  5. Document important conversations.
  6. Escalate when necessary.

Speech Skill Tree

Level 1: Active Listener

Demonstrates attention and understanding before responding.

Level 2: Professional Communicator

Communicates clearly under pressure.

Level 3: Conflict Resolver

Helps others move from frustration toward solutions.

De-Escalation Checklist

Common Workplace Mistakes

  • Responding emotionally
  • Taking criticism personally
  • Making assumptions
  • Interrupting others
  • Ignoring communication problems
