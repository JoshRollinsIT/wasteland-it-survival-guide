Surviving the Team

Building alliances in the corporate wasteland.

Mission Briefing

No IT professional survives alone. Success comes from building relationships, earning trust, and working effectively with others.

"Your technical skills may get you hired. Your teamwork skills help you stay employed."

HR Overseer Advisory

"The employee everyone enjoys working with often advances faster than the employee nobody wants to talk to."

Known Workplace Factions

The Veteran

Has seen every problem imaginable and already knows three ways to fix it.

Survival Strategy

  • Listen more than you speak.
  • Ask thoughtful questions.
  • Learn from their experience.

The Manager

Controls resources, priorities, and deadlines.

Survival Strategy

  • Communicate clearly.
  • Provide updates early.
  • Bring solutions, not only problems.

The Fellow Recruit

New to the workplace and learning the same systems.

Survival Strategy

  • Share knowledge.
  • Support one another.
  • Build professional relationships.

Random Encounter

Wrong Response

"You always mess things up."

Better Response
