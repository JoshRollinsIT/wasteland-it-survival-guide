S.P.E.C.I.A.L Skills

Support

The ability to help users effectively, listen actively, and solve problems professionally.

Professionalism

Being reliable, accountable, and respectful in workplace interactions.

Emotional Intelligence

Recognizing emotions, managing reactions, and communicating with empathy.

Communication

Expressing ideas clearly, listening actively, and building understanding through respectful interaction.

Initiative

Taking action without being asked, identifying opportunities, and following through on solutions.

Accountability

Taking responsibility for actions, meeting commitments, and learning from mistakes.

Learning

Seeking feedback, developing new skills, and continuously improving through experience and education.
