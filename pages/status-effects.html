Status Effects

Protect your mental HP and maintain maximum workplace performance.

Status Effect Briefing

Every IT professional encounters workplace hazards. Some are technical. Others affect your mindset, stress level, and emotional well-being.

"You can't always control what happens in the wasteland, but you can control how you respond."

HR Overseer Advisory

"Ignoring stress doesn't remove it. It only allows it to accumulate."

Known Status Effects

☢ Burnout

Symptoms:

  • Exhaustion
  • Loss of motivation
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Increased frustration

Treatment:

  • Take breaks
  • Use PTO when available
  • Practice healthy work habits
  • Maintain work-life balance

🧠 Imposter Syndrome

Symptoms:

  • Feeling unqualified
  • Fear of asking questions
  • Doubting accomplishments

Treatment:

  • Track achievements
  • Ask questions
  • Remember everyone starts somewhere

⚠ Frustration

Symptoms:

  • Short temper
  • Difficulty focusing
  • Negative reactions

Treatment:

  • Take a short break
  • Breathe before responding
  • Refocus on solutions

Emotional Trigger Detection

Knowing your triggers helps prevent emotional reactions from controlling your behavior.

Common Workplace Triggers

  • Feeling criticized
  • Being interrupted
  • Unexpected changes
  • Heavy workloads
  • Difficult customers

Locus of Control Scanner

Focus your energy on the things you can control.

✅ Things You Can Control

  • Your attitude
  • Your effort
  • Your communication
  • Your preparation

❌ Things You Can't Control

  • Other people's choices
  • Company decisions
  • Unexpected problems
  • Past mistakes
